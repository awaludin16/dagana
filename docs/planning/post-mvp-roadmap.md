# Post-MVP Product Roadmap

> **Status:** Directional roadmap
> **Purpose:** Menyimpan arah pengembangan setelah core MVP stabil. Daftar ini bukan komitmen bahwa seluruh fitur akan dibangun; setiap item membutuhkan prioritas dan requirement tersendiri.

- Quick Sale berdasarkan hasil validasi
- advanced cash management
- improved reports
- transaction search
- receipt customization

#### Phase B — F&B Operations

- recipe/BOM
- advanced inventory
- COGS
- waste
- stock opname
- modifier
- variant

#### Phase C — Ordering & Kitchen

- QR Ordering enhancement
- table management
- kitchen display
- realtime kitchen
- split bill
- multiple payment

#### Phase D — Multi-Outlet

- stock transfer
- centralized catalog
- outlet-specific pricing
- centralized reporting
- central kitchen

#### Phase E — Growth

- loyalty
- promotion
- customer segmentation
- advanced CRM
- accounting integration

#### Phase F — Platform

- advanced billing
- automated billing payment
- owner mobile application
- advanced analytics
- integrations
