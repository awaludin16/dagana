# Product Success & Validation Metrics

> **Status:** Draft
> **Purpose:** Menyimpan indikator untuk mengevaluasi nilai produk dan memvalidasi masalah merchant. Angka target dan formula belum ditetapkan kecuali disepakati secara terpisah.

### Success Metrics

#### Product

- time to first transaction
- activation rate
- active businesses
- active outlets
- transaction completion rate

#### Operational

- average checkout time
- failed transaction rate
- transaction capture rate
- cash variance
- number of reconciliation events

#### Adoption

- percentage of owners using POS directly
- employee activation rate
- repeat selling sessions
- daily active merchants

#### Business

- monthly active businesses
- transactions per business
- processed sales
- billable sales
- platform fee
- merchant retention

---

### Validation Metrics

Karena problem utama masih perlu divalidasi melalui customer interview nyata, Dagana perlu mengukur:

#### Transaction Capture

Berapa banyak transaksi yang benar-benar dicatat saat transaksi terjadi?

#### Cash Variance

Seberapa sering terjadi:

```text
Actual Cash ≠ Expected Cash
```

#### Checkout Speed

Berapa lama merchant menyelesaikan transaksi?

#### Setup Friction

Berapa lama dari:

```text
Register
```

hingga:

```text
First Transaction
```

#### Owner-as-Operator Adoption

Berapa banyak merchant yang:

```text
Owner → POS → Sell
```

tanpa employee?
